When you connect a platform account, you authorise StreamForge to act on your behalf within the permissions you granted at the consent screen — typically reading your basic profile and uploading videos you have approved.

  • Platform APIs change and platforms may suspend access. If a platform stops accepting uploads, StreamForge cannot post to it, and that is outside our control.

4. Local-first operation
