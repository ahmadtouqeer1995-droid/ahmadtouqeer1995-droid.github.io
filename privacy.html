StreamForge — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 August 2026

StreamForge is desktop software that runs on your own computer. This policy explains what it holds, where it holds it, and what leaves your machine.

The short version

Your video, your clips and your credentials stay on your PC. The only data that leaves your computer is what you explicitly publish to a platform you connected, plus the API calls needed to publish it and read back its performance.

1. What StreamForge stores on your computer

  • Recordings and clips — the video and audio you capture, in your chosen media folder.
  • A local database — session records, detected highlight moments and their scores, your posting queue, and the performance metrics fetched for posts you made.
  • Chat messages collected during a live session, used to detect moments when chat reacted. Chat authors are stored as a one-way hash, not as usernames.
  • Configuration — your preferences, destination names and URLs, branding and schedule.

All of this lives on your disk. None of it is uploaded to us. We do not operate a server that receives your recordings.

2. Credentials

Stream keys and platform access tokens are stored in your operating system's credential store (Windows Credential Manager). They are never written to configuration files, never written to logs, and never committed to source control. Log output containing a key or URL is redacted before it is written.

3. What is sent to third parties

Only when you connect an account and approve an action:

  • The video you approved, uploaded to the platform you picked.
  • Its title, description and tags, as you set them.
  • Your basic profile information, read from the platform so the app can show which account it is posting as, and — where a platform provides it — your follower count, used to tell you whether you meet that platform's requirements for going live.
  • Performance metrics for posts you made (views, likes, comments), read back so the app can show how a clip did and improve how it scores future clips.

Each platform's own privacy policy governs what it does with the content once it is uploaded.

4. What is never sent

We do not sell data, run advertising, or share your data with third parties for their own purposes. StreamForge contains no analytics or telemetry that reports your usage back to us.

5. Other people in your content

If your stream captures other people — a co-streamer's voice, a webcam guest, chat participants — you are responsible for having their permission to record and publish that. StreamForge stores chat author identifiers as hashes to limit what a recording reveals about the people in your chat.

6. Retention and deletion

Because everything is local, you control retention:

  • Deleting a recording, clip or project in the app removes it from your disk.
  • Disconnecting a platform account deletes that platform's stored credentials from your credential store.
  • Uninstalling the app and deleting its data folder removes everything it held.

Content you already published to a platform is governed by that platform — delete it there.

7. Children

StreamForge is not intended for use by children, and it should not be used to publish content of children without the consent of a parent or guardian.

8. Changes

This policy may change as the app changes. The "last updated" date shows when.

9. Contact

Questions about privacy: open an issue on the project's repository.
